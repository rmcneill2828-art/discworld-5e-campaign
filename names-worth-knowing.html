Each entry gives you three things to hold onto at the table: what drives them, how they carry themselves, and — most useful mid-scene — how they actually sound. Reading the Tone line out loud a couple of times before a session is usually enough to run the voice convincingly.

These are recurring cast, not statted opposition — treat every fight with any of them as a sign the story has gone somewhere it probably shouldn't.

Lord Vetinari

The Palace

Where: the Patrician's Palace, or wherever he's decided the conversation should happen instead

Personality
Preternaturally patient, sees six moves ahead, believes fear of consequence governs better than law ever could. Cares more than he lets on.
Demeanor
Still. Doesn't fidget, doesn't raise his voice, doesn't need to.
Tone
Precise, economical sentences. Answers questions with questions. Never explains himself twice.
GM TipUse him to redirect a stalled plot without ever giving a direct order — he suggests, and lets the party discover why refusing was the wrong call.

Commander Vimes

City Watch

Where: Pseudopolis Yard

Personality
Cynical about power, incorruptible about people. Drinks less than he used to, and is prouder of that than of anything on his record.
Demeanor
Perpetually tired, perpetually alert — a man doing a job he resents, who'd fight anyone trying to take it from him.
Tone
Blunt, working-class cadence. Distrusts fancy words. Swears when he means it, not for effect.
GM TipGive the party a case he personally cares about, and let his approval — rare, understated — feel like the actual reward.

Archchancellor Ridcully

Unseen University

Where: Unreal Estate, usually mid-lecture or mid-hunt

Personality
Booming and practical, allergic to faculty politics even while charging headfirst into it. Thinks most magical problems have a simple, physical solution.
Demeanor
Loud, outdoorsy, built like he'd rather be hunting than lecturing — and frequently is.
Tone
Hearty, declarative, prone to mixing his metaphors mid-sentence without noticing.
GM TipPerfect for cutting through overcomplicated wizard bureaucracy with a solution the party wouldn't have dared suggest themselves.

The Librarian

Unseen University

Where: the University Library, always

Personality
Patient, protective of the books, entirely unbothered by his own condition. Not a monkey. An orangutan.
Demeanor
Communicates almost entirely through gesture and a single, versatile word, understood by everyone who's been around him for more than a day.
Tone
"Ook," repeated with different emphasis. Tone of voice carries the meaning — never the word choice.
GM TipA great non-verbal puzzle NPC — let players work out what he wants through context and repetition, never subtitle him directly.

Death

Cosmology

Where: everywhere, eventually

Personality
Endlessly curious about mortal life, fond of cats and curry, professionally unsentimental but personally kind.
Demeanor
Perfectly still until he moves — and when he moves, nothing else in the room is more certain.
Tone
SPEAKS IN SMALL CAPITALS. Short, declarative sentences. Never raises his voice, because he's never needed to.
GM TipReserve him for moments that matter — his presence should never feel routine, even though death itself very much is his routine.

Granny Weatherwax

Witch, Ramtops

Where: visiting from Lancre, rarely explains why

Personality
Ferociously principled, allergic to being thanked, more afraid of the harm her own power could do than anything else on the Disc.
Demeanor
Upright, unblinking — the specific authority of someone who has never once needed to raise her voice to be obeyed.
Tone
Short, flat sentences. States things as fact. Doesn't ask questions she already knows the answer to.
GM TipThe model for an unbeatable NPC stare-down — see The Look, the Circle of Headology capstone, for the mechanical version.

Nanny Ogg

Witch, Ramtops

Where: also visiting from Lancre, always explains why, at length

Personality
Warm, shameless, deceptively sharp under the bawdy-song exterior. Runs a family roughly the size of a small army.
Demeanor
Comfortable everywhere, in everyone's business, uninvited and somehow still welcome.
Tone
Familiar, chatty, laced with a joke that gets funnier the third time she tells it. Everyone's "my duck," regardless of size, species, or rank.
GM TipThe warm counterweight to Granny's severity — use her to get information out of an NPC who'd never talk to the Watch directly.

Sergeant Detritus

City Watch

Where: Pseudopolis Yard

Personality
Straightforward, loyal, smarter than his slow, cold-weather delivery suggests once the temperature actually drops.
Demeanor
Enormous. Moves like a landslide that's decided to be polite about it.
Tone
Simple sentence structure that gets noticeably more articulate in cold rooms or cold weather — a fact he'll mention if you ask, and probably even if you don't.
GM TipGreat for an interrogation scene set somewhere cold — let the players notice the shift themselves.

Angua von Uberwald

City Watch

Where: Pseudopolis Yard, or wherever the trail leads

Personality
Competent, guarded, permanently negotiating with her own nature. Better at the job than she'll ever admit.
Demeanor
Composed and watchful, keeping a careful distance until she's decided you're worth trusting.
Tone
Dry, economical, occasionally self-deprecating about the werewolf thing in a way that shuts down further questions.
GM TipUseful for tracking or truth-sensing beats — let her senses catch something the party missed entirely.

Reg Shoe

Civilian

Where: wherever the cause needs him, usually Sator Square with a sign

Personality
Earnest, relentlessly optimistic about undead rights, completely unbothered by his own decomposition.
Demeanor
Enthusiastic in a way that outpaces the room's patience for him — and doesn't notice that it has.
Tone
Speeches. Full, well-structured speeches, delivered whether or not anyone actually asked for one.
GM TipComic relief with real heart — play his cause completely straight even when his delivery isn't.

Cohen the Barbarian

Civilian

Where: passing through, usually the Mended Drum

Personality
A genuine legend, increasingly annoyed that legend-work has gotten so complicated, sharper than his joints have any right to be.
Demeanor
Old. Visibly, audibly old — and still the most dangerous person in most rooms he walks into.
Tone
Gruff, matter-of-fact about violence, mildly baffled by anything invented in the last forty years.
GM TipA living argument that competence beats youth — a strong mentor or rival for a Barbarian or Fighter PC.

C.M.O.T. Dibbler

Civilian, Sator Square

Where: wherever a crowd's gathering, selling something to it

Personality
A confidence man with a genuine, if wildly inconsistent, sense of loyalty to the city he keeps trying to profit from.
Demeanor
Always mid-sales-pitch, even when nobody's buying, even when he's the one being questioned.
Tone
Rapid, salesman patter, always three sentences ahead of whatever's actually true.
GM TipA reliable source of terrible information delivered with total confidence — excellent for red herrings and comic relief.
