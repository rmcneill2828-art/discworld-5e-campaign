# Discworld 5e Campaign

Homebrew reference material for running a Dungeons & Dragons 5th Edition (rules-as-written) campaign set on Terry Pratchett's Discworld.

This repo contains original game-prep material only (setting primers, character options, adventure notes) — no book text, no copyrighted Pratchett content.

## Contents

- [`discworld-charter.html`](discworld-charter.html) — campaign setting primer: cosmology, geography, guilds & factions, magic systems, playable peoples, the Guild/Watch/Civilian origin system, class mapping, and adventure hooks. Also published as a [Claude Artifact](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/e274a93a-d0e7-4a77-9f98-173a41dbd61d).
- [`discworld-guild-backgrounds.html`](discworld-guild-backgrounds.html) — eight 5e RAW Backgrounds (City Watch, five Guilds, Unseen University, Civilian) with skills, tools, equipment, a feature, and roleplay prompts. Companion to the Charter's Origins system. Also published as a [Claude Artifact](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/b26f0bca-c632-4084-8d7a-f9e5981c7654).
- [`circle-of-headology.html`](circle-of-headology.html) — Circle of Headology, a 5e RAW Druid subclass for witches: Headology, Borrowing (replaces Wild Shape), Second Thoughts, Deep Borrowing, and The Look. Also published as a [Claude Artifact](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/25b598ef-1f13-4631-8181-c7a55ce05e2b).
