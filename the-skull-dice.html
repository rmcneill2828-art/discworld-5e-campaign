Character Creation Tables

The Skull Dice

Every character starts in Ankh-Morpork. Why, and how they got there, is a roll of the gods' own dice.

Companion to The Discworld Charter

Somewhere above the Disc, something is shaking a cup made of bone. Nobody's proven it. Nobody who's tried to disprove it has had especially good luck since.

Every character in this campaign starts in Ankh-Morpork, full stop — that part isn't negotiable, the same way the city itself never quite feels negotiable. The why and the how are where the dice come in. Roll once on each table below, or pick freely if a result doesn't fit the character you already have in mind — either way, you'll have the start of a backstory in about ten seconds, and the two results together are usually funnier, sadder, or stranger than anything either of you would have written alone.

How to use this Roll a d20 on Why You're Here, then a d20 on How You Got Here. Read them together as one line. Keep both, swap one, ignore the dice entirely and just pick two that spark something — all three are the intended way to play this. It also doubles as a fast NPC-background generator for the GM between sessions — for a name and a rumor to go with it, pair this with Word on the Street.

7A letter arrived with this address on it and no other explanation.
8You were born here and have simply never left.
9Somewhere else decided it didn't want you anymore.
10You followed someone you were sure you loved. They aren't here now.
11A god you'd never heard of told you, quite clearly, to go.
12You're one dropped coin from a life of crime, and this city has the best rates.
13Someone told you the streets were paved with something valuable. Technically, cobblestones.
14You're hiding from something. It hasn't caught up yet.
15It's the only place your particular skillset is, technically, legal.
16You inherited a building here you've never actually seen.
17The Guild you wanted only has a branch here.
18You were sent to deliver a message and never delivered it, or yourself, elsewhere.
19It's the biggest city on the Disc. You wanted to disappear into a crowd.
20Frankly, no idea. You woke up here and decided that was reason enough.

How You Got Here

d20Method
1Walked. The whole way. You have opinions about boots now.
2Smuggled in the back of a cart, under something you'd rather not describe.
3Worked passage with a merchant caravan, for tasks you're still not sure were legal.
4Rode a barge up the Ankh. You can still smell it.
5Arrived by coach, on time, uneventfully. Suspicious, in retrospect.
6Washed up, literally, after a shipwreck on the coast.
7Stowed away on a ship and were "discovered" around week two.
8Followed a travelling troupe and never left when they did.
9Woke up here with no memory of the journey at all.
10Fled on foot in the middle of the night with whatever you could carry.
11Escorted in by City Watch officers, under circumstances best left undiscussed.
12Won passage in a card game you're fairly sure was rigged, in your favor for once.
13Carried in, unconscious, by someone no longer around to explain why.
14Arrived via a magical mishap, absolutely not your fault. Probably.
15Walked through the gates as a child, holding a hand that let go in the crowd.
16Rode in on a delivery wagon belonging to a Guild you'd never heard of.
17Talked your way onto a diplomatic escort meant for someone far more important.
18Arrived by broom, dragon, or similarly unconventional transport. You'd rather not elaborate.
19Always been here — family roots go back generations, if the records are honest.
20Nobody's entirely sure. Three different accounts exist and you've stopped correcting people.
