The Twelve Districts

Ankh-Morpork divides into twelve named districts fanning out from the river. The thirteen locations below each sit inside one of them — the map after this table shows where.

Sator Square

City Center

The city's largest open plaza, ringed by guildhalls, market stalls, and a scaffold that's seen more practical use than anyone likes to admit. Every parade, proclamation, execution, and unlicensed street performance funnels through here eventually — often all on the same afternoon.

Adventure SeedA public announcement scheduled for noon has been quietly rescheduled three times, and nobody official will say why.
2

The Patrician's Palace

Politics

Home to the Patrician, and to the small army of clerks who keep Ankh-Morpork's machinery of applied paranoia running on schedule. Getting an audience is easy; getting the audience you asked for is another matter entirely.

Adventure SeedA "small errand" arrives with the Patrician's seal, phrased so reasonably that turning it down would require an explanation nobody wants to give.
3

Pseudopolis Yard

City Watch HQ

The Watch's main house — a converted building that still, somehow, looks like it's expecting to be converted into something else. Charge sheets, incident reports, and a noticeboard nobody reads until it's too late to matter.

Adventure SeedA report's gone missing from evidence lockup, and the last name on the sign-out sheet belongs to someone who's been dead for six years.
4

The Shades

Thieves' Guild Territory

Ankh-Morpork's oldest, poorest, and most confidently self-governing district — the Watch patrols it in pairs, if at all, and the Thieves' Guild treats that absence as a professional courtesy. Newcomers are advised to look like they know where they're going, even when they don't.

Adventure SeedSomeone's been robbing people in the Shades without a license, and the Guild wants it stopped before the Watch notices there's a pattern.
5

The Isle of Gods

Temples

A crowded knot of temples and considerably smaller shrines wedged into whatever space was left over, dedicated to gods ranging from genuinely powerful to a lizard the size of a thumb with excellent posture. Rents are steep; belief is steeper.

Adventure SeedA new shrine has appeared overnight, fully built, dedicated to a god nobody has ever heard of — who is asking, very politely, for followers.
6

Unseen University

Wizards

Sprawling, ancient, and administratively byzantine even by University standards, with a library that has opinions about who's allowed inside and a faculty that has opinions about everything else. See the Charter's Cosmology section for how magic actually behaves here.

Adventure SeedAn experiment has gone missing from a locked storeroom and is, worryingly, still drawing a faculty salary.
7

The Assassins' Guild House

Filigree Street

An elegant, expensive, and entirely legitimate finishing school that also, as a separate and clearly invoiced line item, kills people for money. Old stone, older money, immaculate manners.

Adventure SeedA student's final exam contract has gone wrong, and the intended target has hired the party to find out who's still coming.
8

The Guild Quarter

Merchants' & Alchemists' Guilds

A dense cluster of guildhalls, counting-houses, and the occasional smoking crater where an Alchemists' Guild "routine test" went differently than planned. The Merchants' Guild keeps its ledgers here; the Alchemists' Guild keeps its distance from everyone else's windows.

Adventure SeedA shipment logged as "delivered" by the Merchants' Guild never arrived, and the paperwork trail ends at an Alchemists' Guild loading bay.
9

The Fools' Guild Hall

Fools' Guild

A tall, gloomy building where comedy is taught with the joyless rigor of a legal apprenticeship, and every joke on file has been pre-approved, cross-referenced, and drained of anything resembling spontaneity.

Adventure SeedA Guild-licensed joke has started causing genuine offense at court, and the Guild wants to know who filed it and why it passed review.
10

Beggars' Territory

Beggars' Guild

Less a single building than a patchwork of recognized patches spanning half the city, held together by an unwritten and fiercely enforced sense of who's allowed to work which doorway.

Adventure SeedA patch has gone unclaimed for a week after its holder vanished, and three different beggars are quietly building a case for why it should be theirs.
11

The Mended Drum

Tavern

The city's tavern of record for anyone who's arrived from somewhere else with a sword, a grudge, or both. Loud, cheerful, and just dangerous enough to keep things interesting; the staff have seen worse than whatever's about to happen tonight.

Adventure SeedA stranger is buying rounds for the whole tavern and asking, casually, if anyone's free for a job that pays well and explains nothing.
12

Biers

The Undead-Friendly Bar

A quiet, dimly lit establishment that caters — discreetly, and without asking too many questions — to the city's zombies, vampires, and other citizens who keep unusual hours. Reformed and civil; the "reformed" part is a house rule, strictly enforced.

Adventure SeedA Black-Ribboner regular hasn't shown up in three nights, which for someone with no pulse to speak of is genuinely alarming.
13

The Docks

Trade & Arrivals

Where the River Ankh finally does something useful, disgorging goods, travelers, and the occasional diplomatic incident from across the Circle Sea. Customs officers work in shifts specifically so no one has to smell the water for too long.

Adventure SeedA ship's manifest and its actual cargo disagree with each other in ways that suggest one of them is lying on purpose.
